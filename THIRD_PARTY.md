## Referenced frameworks and datasets

The detection rules, the MASVS/MASWE taxonomy, the MITRE tags and the tracker and spyware database
(`Sources/ScannerKit/Resources/Rules/`) are built from the public sources below: categories,
technique IDs, documented behaviour and published indicators. They require attribution only.

`CitationTests` fails if a rule cites nothing, cites a source missing from
[`taxonomy/sources.json`](Sources/ScannerKit/Resources/Rules/taxonomy/sources.json), or cites an
Android MASTG test. The table below is generated by `ISAT --sources-report`, and
`ISAT --sources-report --check` fails when it is out of date.

The tracker database (`Rules/trackers/trackers.json`) is maintained by hand from public SDK
documentation, vendor domains and the FTC and research sources above. It is not imported from a
third-party tracker list.

## Sourcing policy

When extending the tracker database or the rules, only use material whose licence allows
modification and redistribution (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, MPL), and record where it came from here.
